## Interpretation

The held-out validation NDCG@10 = 0.7997 is good — the model generally identifies fast kernels — but a significant train/val gap (train NDCG@10 ≈ 0.97 vs val ≈ 0.80) indicates **overfitting**. The model memorizes per-kernel patterns rather than generalizing fully to unseen kernels.

The most impactful next steps are:

1. **Early stopping** — add `early_stopping_rounds=50` to the training callbacks; the val curve plateaus around round 100 and slightly degrades after round 250.
2. **Stronger regularization** — increase `min_child_samples` (e.g., 20→50), add `reg_lambda=1.0` to reduce leaf-level overfitting.
3. **More diverse kernels** — the dataset covers only 117 distinct kernel shapes; expanding op variety (e.g., more conv configs, attention variants) would reduce the gap.

**Per-kernel outliers**: Five kernels (`0349d1cc`, `768acfd6`, `9b30d1c4`, `fdde285c`, `de2263ac`) all score NDCG@10 = 0.6309 — suspiciously uniform, suggesting these are single-group kernels where the ranking is partially degenerate. `83c7c223` (9 groups) scores 0.7053 and warrants investigation.
