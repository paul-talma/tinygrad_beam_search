## Interpretation

Regularization and early stopping reduced the train/val gap (train NDCG@10: 0.926 vs 0.974 previously) but did **not** move the val ceiling — it stayed at ~0.80. This confirms the bottleneck is **data diversity**, not overfitting: the dataset covers only 117 distinct kernel shapes, and the 10% held-out split withholds ~12 kernels the model has never seen. No amount of regularization can help a model generalize to structurally novel kernels it has no training signal for.

The five single-group kernels (`conv_5x5_b4`, `conv_medium` ×2, `matmul_16384`, `matmul_1024`) still score NDCG@10 = 0.6309 — these have exactly one beam step of data each, making within-group ranking partially degenerate regardless of model quality.

**Primary recommendation**: collect more diverse kernel shapes (more conv configs, varying batch sizes, fused ops) rather than further tuning regularization.
